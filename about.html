About Ubuntu Paws Animal Rescue

Our history

Ubuntu Paws Animal Rescue was founded in 2019 by a small group of animal-welfare volunteers who began by fostering abandoned animals from their own homes. As demand grew, the group established a small shelter in Johannesburg and built a network of foster carers to extend its reach across Gauteng. Since then, the organisation has rehomed over 400 animals.

Our mission

To reduce animal suffering in Johannesburg through rescue, rehabilitation and community education.

Our vision

A community where no companion animal is homeless or neglected.
